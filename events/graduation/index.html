About

Graduation is the one occasion here that isn't a date on a calendar: it happens when it happens, and the words have to carry a name, a year, and a degree. That shapes what this page is for: styling "Class of 2027", a full name for an announcement, or a congratulations message for a card. It gathers curated script and bold fonts you can preview on your own text, the cap, scroll, and star emoji people actually paste, kaomoji, a few original ASCII art pieces, and a bank of congratulations messages: for your own child, a friend, a student, or a colleague.

Graduation: in spring and early summer in most countries, though ceremonies run year-round
