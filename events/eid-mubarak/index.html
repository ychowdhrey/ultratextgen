Eid Mubarak marks two of the biggest greeting-and-gifting occasions on the Islamic lunar calendar: Eid al-Fitr, celebrated at the end of Ramadan, and Eid al-Adha, marked during the Hajj season a couple of months later. Both are celebrated with prayer, family gatherings, sweets, and messages wishing loved ones a blessed holiday.
