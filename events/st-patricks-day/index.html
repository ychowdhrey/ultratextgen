About

St Patrick's Day runs on a small, well-worn set of words: a blessing, a toast, and a joke about being pinched for not wearing green. This page gathers everything for styling them: curated Celtic-leaning and bold fonts you can preview on your own text, the shamrock, clover, and rainbow emoji people actually paste, Celtic knot and cross symbols, kaomoji, a few original ASCII art pieces, and a bank of Irish blessings, toasts, and captions, from the full "May the road rise up to meet you" to a two-word story caption.
