Threads Font Basics

Threads has no built-in way to change fonts — there's no bold button, no italic option, and no text formatting menu anywhere in the app. The workaround is Unicode text: characters that look like bold, cursive, or gothic letters but copy and paste like normal text.

Step-by-step
1. Type or paste your text into the generator above
2. Browse the styles — bold, italic, cursive, gothic, bubble, and more
3. Tap Copy on the style you like
4. Paste it into your Threads post, reply, or bio

No downloads, no extensions — it works straight from your browser on desktop and mobile.

Compatibility and Accessibility

The core styles — bold, italic, cursive, gothic, bubble — are widely supported and render correctly on modern phones and computers. A few rare decorative styles use characters that older devices may not include; on those devices they can show up as boxes (▯). If a style looks broken in your own preview, pick an alternative — the popular styles are the safe ones.
They can be, when overused — worth knowing before you style a whole post. Screen readers often announce styled Unicode letter-by-letter or skip it entirely, which makes long styled passages difficult for visually impaired followers. Styled text also doesn't match plain-text search: 𝗵𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗼 won't match a search for "hello" on Threads or on Google.

Best practice: use styled text as an accent — your name, a short headline, a key word — and keep the body of your posts and anything you want to be searchable in plain text.
