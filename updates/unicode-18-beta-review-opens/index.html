Unicode 18.0 Beta Review Opens — Here's What's Shipping

The Unicode Consortium opened public beta review for Unicode 18.0 on May 26, 2026. It adds 13,047 new characters — four historical scripts, three currency symbols, and a draft slate of 9 new emoji. Here's everything confirmed, and what's still pending before the September 2026 final release.

Unicode 18.0 Beta opened May 26, 2026 13,047 new characters
What shipped

13,047 New Characters, Mostly From Four Historical Scripts

On May 26, 2026, the Unicode Consortium opened the public beta review for Unicode 18.0, following February 2026's alpha review. The draft repertoire adds 13,047 characters, bringing the total encoded repertoire to 172,848 characters once finalized.

About 90% of that growth is historical writing systems, not new emoji, spread across four new scripts: Small Seal (11,328 ideographs, an ancient Chinese seal-script form), Jurchen (914 characters, the historic ideographic script used in northeastern China during the Jin and Ming dynasties), Proto-Cuneiform (323 numeric characters, among the earliest writing systems in human history), and Chisoi (40 characters, a modern script used for the Kurmali language in northeast India).

The rest of the release is what most people will actually notice: three new currency symbols (Maldivian rufiyaa, Omani rial, and UAE dirham), 72 historical mathematical symbols, 73 musical symbols, and a draft slate of 9 new emoji.

Where Unicode 18.0 Actually Stands

"Beta" and "final" are different milestones, and "final" and "on your device" are two more after that. Here's the full sequence and where it currently sits.

The character repertoire is considered "stable" at beta — characters could still be modified or removed, but that would need strong justification this late in the process.

What's next

Final Release Expected September 2026 — Then the Emoji Slate Gets Interesting

Unicode 18.0 is expected to publish as final in September 2026, alongside its emoji companion release, Emoji 18.0. The scripts and symbols above are largely locked in at this stage; the one part of this release still worth watching closely is the 9-emoji draft slate — Cracking Face, Pickle, Meteor, and the rest — which we've broken out symbol by symbol, including which ones are winning the public vote, in a companion entry. We'll update this page once Unicode 18.0 itself finalizes.
