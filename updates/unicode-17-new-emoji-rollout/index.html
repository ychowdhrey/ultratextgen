Unicode 17.0's New Emoji: Where They Work Right Now

8 new emoji shipped in Unicode 17.0. Here's what they are, and a platform-by-platform tracker of who actually has them yet — updated as availability changes.

Unicode 17.0 Published 2026-07-20 Rollout tracker
What shipped

8 New Emoji, Published September 9, 2025

The Unicode Consortium published Unicode 17.0 — and with it, Emoji 17.0 — on September 9, 2025. It added eight new emoji to the standard: Distorted Face, Fight Cloud, Hairy Creature (Bigfoot), Orca, Landslide, Trombone, Treasure Chest, and Ballet Dancer.

Publishing the standard is only step one. Unicode.org defines the character; each operating system and app then has to ship its own font and keyboard update before that emoji actually renders for its users — which is why "new emoji released" and "new emoji shows up on your phone" can be months apart, and why this page exists as a tracker rather than a one-time announcement.

The check

Platform Rollout Status

Apple shipped first. The Unicode 17.0 emoji reached iPhone in iOS 26.4 on March 24, 2026, and by June 1, 2026 were live across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Android, Windows, Samsung, and WhatsApp are rolling the set out through 2026 on their own separate schedules — none of them publish a single fixed completion date, so "rolling out" below means exactly that: in progress, not yet complete everywhere.

Why it matters here

A Blank Box Isn't Always a Mistake

Every "Check" tool on this site — the game-name checker, the platform compatibility answers — depends on the same underlying fact: whether a character renders depends on what the reader's device supports, not just what the standard defines. A brand-new Unicode 17.0 emoji showing as an empty box on an older Android phone is the same failure mode covered in why fonts show as boxes — it just has a different, very specific cause: the platform hasn't shipped this particular Unicode version yet.

What's next

Unicode 18.0 Is Already Planned

The next version, Unicode 18.0, is tentatively planned for September 2026. Its draft repertoire adds roughly 13,000 new characters — but the overwhelming majority of that is a single new script (over 11,000 Small Seal ideographs), not a fresh batch of emoji. We'll post a tracker like this one once it ships and platforms start rolling it out.
