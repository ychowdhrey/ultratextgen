Text Decorator Basics

A text decorator is a free online tool that adds decorative symbols, emoji, borders, and brackets around your text. For example, it can turn "Hello World" into ★Hello★ ★World★, ꧁Hello World꧂, or ❄️Hello❄️ ❄️World❄️.

Unlike fancy font generators that restyle individual letters, a text decorator keeps your letters readable and decorates around them — perfect for bios, captions, usernames, and announcements. No downloads or sign-ups needed.
Type or paste your text, pick a vibe (Sparkle, Hearts, Coquette, Kawaii, Gaming, Gothic, Celestial, and more), and the decorator instantly generates dozens of decorated versions — wrappers, stacked symbols, banners, and word rhythms. Hit Shuffle for a completely fresh set, decorate the whole text, each word, or each letter, and dial the intensity from Light to Extra. Or drop in your own symbol — your country's flag, a star, anything — and the decorator builds every style around it (🇵🇰 Y 🇵🇰 A 🇵🇰 S 🇵🇰 I 🇵🇰 R 🇵🇰).

Click copy on any result and paste it directly into Instagram, TikTok, Twitter (X), WhatsApp, Discord, or any other platform. No account or download required. If you'd rather copy ready-made symbols one by one, browse our aesthetic symbols and borders & frames libraries.
The text decorator applies symbols and decorations around whole words for a grouped, attention-grabbing effect (e.g., ❄️Winter❄️ ❄️Sale❄️). Bubble Fonts enclose each individual character in a circle (e.g., Ⓦⓞⓡⓓ), and Fancy Fonts change the style of each letter using Unicode.

Decoration is ideal for bold, word-level emphasis, while the other styles transform the characters themselves — and you can combine both.

Bracket text symbols wrap each word in characters like [ ], < >, or { } to create stylish, structured-looking text. For example, "Hello World" becomes [Hello] [World] or {Hello} {World}.

Use the decorator by selecting a bracket-style wrapper, typing your text, and copying the result. They're popular for social media bios, gaming usernames, and aesthetic captions. See the full set of Unicode brackets in our bracket symbols library.
In text styling, brackets are primarily decorative — they add visual emphasis or a structured aesthetic without changing the meaning of your words. They're popular for creating clean, bold-looking bios and usernames.

Outside of styling, brackets have traditional uses like editorial corrections ([sic] in quotes) or indicating optional content. In text decoration, they're all about the look.

Accessibility

Screen readers typically read the underlying plain text and skip decorative symbols. However, heavily decorated text can sometimes affect readability for assistive technology users.

If accessibility matters for your audience, consider providing a plain-text version alongside your styled post.
