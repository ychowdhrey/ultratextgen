Fullwidth & Vaporwave Tips

Fullwidth text uses Unicode “fullwidth” forms of normal letters and numbers. Each character takes a full CJK-width cell, which creates the wide, spaced-out vaporwave look.
Yes. Fullwidth characters are part of core Unicode and render on almost every platform, including Instagram, TikTok, X, WhatsApp, and Discord.
The wide spacing became a signature of vaporwave aesthetics online, so fullwidth text is often searched for as “vaporwave” or “aesthetic” text.
