Emoji Letter Tips

Emoji letters are Unicode symbols shaped like squared or blocked letters, plus regional-indicator letters. They render as small tiles or boxes rather than plain text.
On their own, two regional-indicator letters can combine into a flag. This generator inserts an invisible separator so each letter stays a separate boxed letter instead of forming a flag.
Emoji-based letters work well on Instagram, X, Discord, and most modern apps. On older systems some may fall back to plain outlines.
