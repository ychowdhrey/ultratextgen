How to Change Your Name in Free Fire

To change your Free Fire nickname you need a Name Change Card. Here are the steps:

1. Copy your styled name from the generator or the name lists above.
2. Open Free Fire → tap your profile picture in the top-left corner → profile icon.
3. Tap the rename button (the pencil icon next to your nickname).
4. Paste the copied name, then confirm with a Name Change Card (new accounts usually get one free card the first time; after that you buy it with diamonds).

Because every letter and symbol is its own Unicode character, the styling won't disappear when you paste it — unlike regular bold formatting in chat apps that reverts to plain text the moment you copy it.

Free Fire Names with Spaces & Names That Aren't Taken

Using spaces: Free Fire doesn't accept a normal space in a nickname. The trick is an invisible character. Copy the blank character from our Invisible Character page and paste it between two words — your name looks like it has a space even though it's filled with a special Unicode character.

Keeping it unique: combining your name + a font style + symbols makes your nickname one of a kind. Instead of a plain name that's easy to duplicate, wrap it in umbrellas ꧁꧂, switch the letters to small caps or gothic, and slip in one or two rare symbols. The more character variety, the less likely someone already has that name.
