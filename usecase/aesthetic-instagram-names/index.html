How to Change Your Name on Instagram

Instagram actually has two separate fields, and only one of them accepts styled fonts and symbols:

Name (display field): open the app, go to your profile, tap Edit Profile, tap the Name field, paste your styled name, and confirm. This field is free text — Unicode fonts, symbols, and emoji all display fine.
@username: also editable from Edit Profile, but Instagram restricts it to plain lowercase letters, numbers, periods, and underscores — decorative fonts and symbols won't work here, and Instagram limits how often you can change it.

Copy your styled name from the generator or the lists above, and use it for the Name field, not the @username.
