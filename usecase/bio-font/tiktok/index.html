Character Limits & Rules

80 characters — the counter above already matches this limit with TikTok selected. It's one of the tightest bio limits of any major platform, so most people style a single anchor line rather than a full sentence.
Usually yes, and often as two characters, not one. TikTok counts by UTF-16 code units, and most emoji (and many styled Unicode letters) are made of two code units, so a bio that looks like 60 characters can quietly use closer to 80. If the counter above turns red, you're over — trim a symbol or shorten a word rather than guessing.
