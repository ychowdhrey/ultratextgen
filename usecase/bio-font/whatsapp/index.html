Rendering & Safety

Mostly, yes — WhatsApp renders standard Unicode consistently across iOS and Android. Very old devices or heavily decorative styles (like Fraktur/Gothic or zalgo) are the most likely to show as boxes on someone's screen. Bold, Italic, and Small Caps are the safest, near-universal choices. See Why Text Shows as Boxes (Tofu) for the full safety tier list.
