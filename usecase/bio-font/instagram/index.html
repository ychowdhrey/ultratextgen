Character Limits & Rules

150 characters for the bio field — the counter above already matches this limit with Instagram selected. That's a separate, tighter limit from your username, which caps at 30 characters and only accepts lowercase letters, numbers, periods and underscores. Unicode fonts don't work in the username field, only in the bio and display name — see our username character rules for the full breakdown.
Usually yes, and often as two characters, not one. Instagram counts by UTF-16 code units, and most emoji (and many styled Unicode letters) are made of two code units, so a bio that looks like 120 characters can quietly use closer to 150. Style & Aesthetic Ideas

Ultra Script Bold — an elegant cursive that reads as curated rather than try-hard — is the single most-used style in Instagram bios. Ultra Bold works well for a name or one anchor line, and Ultra Bubble suits softer, playful profiles. Font is only half of it — most aesthetic bios follow a short structure: name, then a divider, then one or two descriptors (pronouns, vibe, interest), e.g. "✦ Maya ⋆ she/her ⋆ dreamer ✦".
