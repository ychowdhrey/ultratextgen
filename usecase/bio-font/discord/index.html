Character Limits & Rules

About Me supports up to 190 characters of full Unicode — styled fonts, symbols and emoji all work. That's different from your username, which allows no Unicode at all (lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and periods only). See the full field-by-field breakdown at Discord Allowed Characters: Username, Display Name, Server & Channel Rules.

Rendering & Safety

It's a device font-support gap, not a Discord rule — some Unicode blocks aren't shipped in every phone's font set, so a style that looks perfect on your screen can render as □□□□ on someone else's, especially older Android devices. Bold, italic and small caps are the safest, near-universal choices; Fraktur (blackletter/Gothic) and heavily decorative or zalgo styles are the most likely to break. See Why Text Shows as Boxes (Tofu) for the full safety tier list.
No, ordinary styled Unicode text is not against Discord's platform rules. The one styling choice that does get moderated in practice is heavy zalgo (many stacked combining marks), because it can break chat layout and readability — many servers restrict it in their own rules even though it isn't a platform-wide ban. Keep decoration at the edges of a name or line rather than stacked through it. More detail: Style a Discord Name Without Getting Filtered.
