How to Change Your Mobile Legends Squad Name

Usually only the squad leader can change the squad name. Here are the steps:

1. Copy your cool squad name from the Tag Studio or the name ideas above.
2. Open Mobile Legends → Squad menu → squad settings/info.
3. Choose the change-squad-name option.
4. Paste the copied name and confirm.

Because the characters are Unicode, the style won't disappear when you paste it. But remember the squad name field is short and Unicode fonts sometimes count as longer than they look — check that the name isn't cut off or rejected before you confirm.

Abbreviation Tips & Pairing a Name with a Logo

A squad abbreviation hits hardest when it's short and means something — pull it from your team's initials, an animal, or a word that's easy to shout out during a match. Three to five letters is the sweet spot so it fits and stays readable on the leaderboard.

A squad name is text (what you build here), while a squad logo is an emblem you pick from in-game templates. The two hit hardest when they match.
