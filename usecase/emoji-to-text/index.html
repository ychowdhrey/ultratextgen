Emoji to Text — FAQ

An emoji to text converter does the reverse of a text-to-emoji tool: you paste a string of emoji and it decodes each one into the closest plain-English word. For example, 😴🍕🎬 becomes "sleep pizza movie". It runs instantly in your browser.
Paste or type emoji into the box and the converter reads each emoji grapheme, looks up its meaning, and returns readable words separated by spaces. It updates live as you type — no button needed.
It covers the most common, widely-used emoji — faces, food, animals, objects, nature, and symbols. Rare, brand-new, or highly abstract emoji may not have a single-word match and are skipped.
Yes — completely free, with no sign-up. Decode as much as you like and copy the result to paste anywhere.
Use the text to emoji converter for the other direction — it turns your words into emoji, or spells names in emoji letters. The mode tabs above link straight to each tool.
