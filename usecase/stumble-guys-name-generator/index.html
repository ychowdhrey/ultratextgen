How to Change Your Name in Stumble Guys

To rename in Stumble Guys:

1. Copy your styled name from the generator or the name lists above.
2. Open Stumble Guys and tap your profile icon.
3. Tap the pencil/edit icon next to your current name.
4. Paste the copied name and confirm — it must be 4 to 12 characters.

Your first rename is free. Every rename after that costs 100 gems, so it's worth checking the name fits before you confirm — the live checker above counts it for you.

Do Symbols Work in Stumble Guys Names?

The official rename box form-validates against some special characters, but players widely report that styled Unicode letters — small caps, and Cyrillic or Greek look-alike letters — paste through cleanly, along with common decorative symbols like stars ✦, bolts ⚡, and gaming frames ꧁꧂. Because this can vary by device, the checker above flags anything untested rather than promising it will always work — safer to test a style before spending a rename.
