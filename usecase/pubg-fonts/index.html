How to Change Your Name in PUBG Mobile

To change your PUBG Mobile nickname you need a Rename Card. Here are the steps:

1. Copy your styled name from the generator or the name lists above.
2. Open PUBG Mobile → tap your profile picture in the top-left → the profile screen.
3. Tap the pencil/rename icon next to your current name.
4. Paste the copied name, then confirm with a Rename Card (new accounts usually get one free card the first time; after that you buy it with UC).

Because every letter and symbol is its own Unicode character, the styling won't disappear when you paste it — unlike regular bold formatting in chat apps that reverts to plain text the moment you copy it.

PUBG Names with Spaces & Names That Aren't Taken

Using spaces: PUBG Mobile doesn't accept a normal space in a nickname. The trick is an invisible character. Copy the blank character from our Invisible Character page and paste it between two words — your name looks like it has a space even though it's filled with a special Unicode character. The PUBG flair tab above also has a space-fill option that inserts this invisible character for you.

Keeping it unique: combining your name + a font style + symbols makes your nickname one of a kind. Instead of a plain name that's easy to duplicate, wrap it in umbrellas ꧁꧂, switch the letters to small caps or gothic, and slip in one or two rare symbols. The more character variety, the less likely someone already has that name.
