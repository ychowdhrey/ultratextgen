How to Add a Matching Clan Tag to Your Free Fire Name

First, agree on the tag with your squad — this only works if everyone applies the same one. Build it above: 3–6 letters, a font (Bold and Small Caps read most reliably), and a frame like ꧁꧂ or [ ].

Then in Free Fire: open your Profile, tap the edit-name (pencil) icon, and paste the tag at the very front of your existing nickname — for example [FOX]Player. Save. Because the free-name-change is one-time, changing it again later usually costs a Nickname Card (paid with diamonds) — decide on the tag before the whole squad commits.

Finally, hit Share Team Template above and drop the link in your group chat. Each teammate opens it, types their own name, and the identical font and frame apply automatically — they just add their own name after the tag and paste it in.

Clan Tag vs. Guild Name — What's the Difference?

These are three separate things, and it's easy to mix them up. Your nickname is your own personal in-game name — see the Free Fire Name Generator if you just want to style that. Your clan tag (this page) is a short prefix every member manually adds to the front of their own nickname, so the squad matches wherever names are visible in a match or lobby — Free Fire has no separate field for it, it's purely a player convention.

Your Guild Name is different again: it's the guild's own display name, set once by the guild leader inside Guild Settings, and it mostly shows up inside the guild's own menus rather than next to you in a match. If you want to build that instead, see the Free Fire Guild Name Generator.
