Emoji Letters — FAQ

Emoji letters turn each character of your text into a boxed emoji letter (a regional-indicator symbol), so SAM becomes 🇸 🇦 🇲. They're a fun, eye-catching way to write names and short words.
Type your name in the box and it's spelled instantly in emoji letters. Copy it into a bio, gamertag, username, or Discord channel name.
Yes — they're standard Unicode. A space is placed between each letter so two letters never merge into a country flag, which keeps names readable across apps. Exact appearance varies slightly by device.
Yes — free and instant, with no sign-up. Spell as many names or words as you like and copy the result to paste anywhere.
