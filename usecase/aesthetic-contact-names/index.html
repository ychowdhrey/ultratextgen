How to Rename a Contact on iPhone & Android

Copy your styled name from the generator or the lists above, then:

iPhone: open Contacts, tap the contact, tap Edit, tap the First Name (or Nickname) field, paste, then tap Done.
Android: open Contacts, tap the contact, tap the pencil/edit icon, tap the name field, paste, then tap Save.

Both apps store names as plain Unicode text, so the styling stays exactly as it appeared here — no reformatting, no missing characters.
