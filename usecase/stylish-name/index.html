FF Name Style: Changing Your Name in Free Fire & BGMI

To use a stylish name in Free Fire you need a Name Change Card — new accounts usually get one free; after that it costs 390 diamonds in the in-game store. Copy your styled name from this page, open Free Fire → tap your profile in the top-left → tap the pencil (rename) icon → paste and confirm. In BGMI / PUBG Mobile the same job needs a Rename Card from your inventory (one is given free early on; more come from events and Royale Pass rewards).

Two rules trip most players up. First, Free Fire's limit is 12 characters and decorative symbols count as 2 each — so ꧁KING꧂ already uses 8 of the 12. Second, a plain space is rejected; players fill the gap with an invisible character instead. The name checker above counts your name exactly the way the game does, so verify before you spend a card. For a deeper FF-focused tool with guild names and the space trick explained step by step, use the Free Fire name generator.

If a letter of your name shows as a box ▯ in-game, the phone or game font simply doesn't include that rare character — switch to a safer style like Bold or Small Caps and keep to the popular symbols on this page (꧁ ꧂ ༒ メ 彡 ★ ™), which render almost everywhere.

Stylish Name for Instagram & Facebook

The same Unicode fonts work outside games too: paste a stylish name into your Instagram bio, your display name, comments, and captions, or into your Facebook bio, posts, and comments — and into your WhatsApp profile name and status. Because the styling is real text, it survives copy paste and shows up for everyone without any app.

One important caution: the @username (handle) field is different. Instagram and Facebook usernames only accept plain letters, numbers, dots, and underscores — fancy fonts pasted there are rejected. Stylish text belongs in the display name and bio, not the handle. We explain exactly why in why Instagram won't accept a fancy username, and if your goal is a full styled bio rather than a name, the bio font generator is built for that.
