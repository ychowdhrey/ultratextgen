The guild name field is narrower, so a 3–5 letter tag plus one or two symbols usually fits best and stays readable in the guild list. How to Change Your Free Fire Guild Name

Only the guild leader (or a co-leader with permission) can change the guild name. Here are the steps:

1. Copy your styled guild name from the generator or the name ideas above.
2. Open Free Fire → Guild menu → guild settings icon.
3. Choose the change-guild-name option.
4. Paste the copied name and confirm (renaming a guild usually costs diamonds).

Because the characters are Unicode, the style won't disappear when you paste it. But remember the guild name field is short, so check that the name isn't cut off before you confirm.

Pair Your Guild Name with a Logo

A guild name is text (what you build here), while a guild logo is an emblem you pick from in-game templates. A squad identity hits hardest when the two match — for example a fire-themed name (🔥 ࿐) with a red logo, or a royalty-themed name (꧁ ♛) with a crown logo.

For the base name, try bold fonts for something firm, gothic fonts for a darker look, or small caps for a clean style that stays readable at a small size. Want to decorate your personal nickname too? Head to Free Fire Name Generator.
