How to Change Your Name in Mobile Legends

Renaming in MLBB costs a Rename Card, so it's worth getting right the first time:

1. Copy your styled name from the generator or the name lists above.
2. Open Mobile Legends and tap your profile avatar.
3. Tap the edit control next to your current name.
4. Paste the copied name and confirm — it must be 4 to 16 characters.

New accounts get one free Rename Card; after that they're bought from the shop with diamonds or earned from events. The card is spent on confirmation, so use the live checker above to see the real character count of the decorated name before you commit.

Which Symbols Work in Mobile Legends Names

MLBB's name field is friendlier to decoration than most. The Javanese umbrella frames ꧁ ꧂ are the signature look, usually paired with ༒ or ࿐ just inside them. Crowns, stars, and the Japanese-style ornaments 彡 メ render fine too, as do all the styled Unicode letter sets — bold, cursive, gothic (Fraktur), and small caps.

Two things to keep in mind. MLBB does accept plain spaces, which most battle-royale name fields don't — so a two-word name needs no separator trick. And the 4-character minimum is real: very short names get rejected outright, which is a distinct failure from being too long.
