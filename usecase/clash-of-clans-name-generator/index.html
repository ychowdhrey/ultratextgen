How to Change Your Name in Clash of Clans

To rename in Clash of Clans:

1. Copy your styled name from the generator or the name lists above.
2. Open Clash of Clans and tap the Settings (gear) icon.
3. Tap "Change Name."
4. Paste the copied name and confirm — it must be 2 to 15 characters.

Unlike some games, Clash of Clans doesn't give you free renames — you'll need a Name Change card, earned from the Trader, Gold Pass, or special offers, and each one can only be used once. Worth checking the name fits before you confirm — the live checker above counts it for you.

Do Symbols Work in Clash of Clans Names?

Clash of Clans' rename box accepts a wide range of Unicode text, and players widely report that styled Unicode letters — small caps, and Cyrillic or Greek look-alike letters — paste through cleanly, along with common decorative symbols like crowns ♛, swords ⚔, and gaming frames ꧁꧂. Because this can vary by device, the checker above flags anything untested rather than promising it will always work — safer to test a style before spending a Name Change card.
