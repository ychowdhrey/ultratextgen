The Sagittarius symbol (♐, U+2650) is an arrow crossing a bow. The most common myth behind it is the centaur Chiron — tutor to the hero Achilles, who traded away his own immortality to free Prometheus and was placed among the stars by Zeus in gratitude (a rival account from the ancient astronomer Eratosthenes instead names the satyr Crotus, credited as the inventor of archery). Independent of the mythology, the constellation has a real astronomical claim to fame: its ‘Teapot’ star pattern points almost directly at the center of the Milky Way, the location of the supermassive black hole Sagittarius A*. Sagittarius runs November 22–December 21, is ruled by Jupiter, and is a Fire sign with Mutable modality.
