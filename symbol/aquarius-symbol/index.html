Aquarius Symbol

Aquarius's glyph (♒) is two wavy lines for flowing water — yet despite the name and the imagery, Aquarius is classified as an Air sign, a mismatch unique among the whole zodiac. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The Aquarius symbol (♒, U+2652) is drawn as two parallel wavy lines representing flowing water. Its myth is Ganymede, a youth abducted by Zeus (in the form of an eagle) to serve as cupbearer to the gods on Olympus — a figure that traces back even further to Enki (Ea), the Mesopotamian god of water and wisdom. Despite the water-bearer name and the wave-shaped glyph, Aquarius is actually classified as an Air sign, not a Water sign — a genuine mismatch unique among the twelve. Aquarius runs January 20–February 18, is ruled by Uranus in modern astrology (traditionally Saturn), and has Fixed modality.
