New Year Wishes & Messages: What to Write

Short, funny, heartfelt, and professional lines for a card, a text, or a caption — pick one, tweak it, send it.

Short answer

The safest all-purpose message is a variation on "Happy New Year — wishing you health, happiness, and new beginnings." It names the occasion, wishes something positive, and works whether you're texting a friend, signing a card, or captioning a photo. From there, adjust the length and tone to the channel: shorter and lighter for a text or DM, warmer for a card, and more restrained for a work message.

The whole method in one line: name the occasion · wish something specific · match the length to the channel.
Texts & DMs

Short New Year wishes for a text or DM

For a text or DM, brevity reads as sincerity — a long paragraph at midnight feels like a form letter. A single line is enough:

"Happy New Year! 🎉"

"See you on the other side of midnight"

"New year, new us — happy 2027"

"Cheers to everything we didn't finish this year"

Cards

What to write in a New Year's card

A card has room for three short parts, each about one sentence: a greeting, a warm wish for the year ahead, and a closing line.

Greeting: "Happy New Year!"
Wish: "Wishing you health, happiness, and a little more adventure this year."
Closing: "Cheers to us" or "With love, always."
Work & Professional

A professional New Year message for colleagues or clients

Keep work messages warm but restrained — gratitude plus forward motion, nothing personal or jokey:

"Wishing you a happy and successful New Year — looking forward to working together in 2027."

Avoid emoji-heavy phrasing or party-toast language in a client email; save the fireworks and confetti for the group chat.

Funny & Casual

Funny New Year lines for close friends

"My New Year's resolution is to stop making New Year's resolutions."

"May your Wi-Fi be strong and your coffee be stronger this year."

"Here's to a year with fewer typos and more naps."
