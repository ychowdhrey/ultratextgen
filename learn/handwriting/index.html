How this guide works

Handwriting is learned in stages, and most frustration comes from practicing the wrong stage — worksheets before readiness, blank lines before the tracing has faded, cursive before print is settled. This guide walks the stages in order. Each chapter explains the why in plain language, then points at the exact free tool on this site that practices it: the worksheet generator with its seven support levels and stroke-direction arrows, name tracing, the cursive alphabet with all 26 letter guides, and the dot-to-dot alphabet for pencil control.
