Wink Kaomoji

Copy and paste wink kaomoji — playful, cheeky Japanese text faces with one eye closed. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Wink kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces with one eye shut in a playful wink — flirty, cheeky, or just cute. A wink kaomoji such as (^_-) or (･ω<)☆ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and chats. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "wink kaomoji" or "kaomoji wink," every face here is free to copy and paste.
