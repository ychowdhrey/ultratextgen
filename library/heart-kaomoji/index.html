Heart Kaomoji

Copy and paste heart kaomoji — cute Japanese text faces carrying hearts and heart eyes. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Heart kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built around the heart symbol ♥ — heart-shaped eyes, faces holding out a heart, and little hearts floating around a smile. A heart kaomoji such as (♥ω♥) or (づ￣ ³￣)づ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and messages. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "heart kaomoji" or "kaomoji heart," every face here is free to copy and paste.
