Heart Kaomoji

Copy and paste heart kaomoji — cute Japanese text faces with hearts, heart eyes, and blown kisses. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Heart kaomoji (also spelled heart kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces that carry a ♥ or ♡ — for affection, crushes, and love notes. A heart kaomoji like (♥ω♥) or (´｡• ᵕ •｡`)♡ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames.
