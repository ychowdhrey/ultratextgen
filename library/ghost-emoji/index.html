Ghost Emoji

Every ghost and apparition emoji, the Halloween cast it haunts with, the 'ghosting' dating-slang symbols, and spooky kaomoji. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The ghost emoji 👻 lives two lives. During spooky season it's a friendly sheet-ghost for Halloween costumes, haunted-house captions, and trick-or-treat posts, right alongside the 🎃 jack-o-lantern and 🦇 bat. The rest of the year, 👻 carries a second, unrelated meaning: ghosting — cutting off contact and disappearing on someone without any explanation, whether that's a match who stopped replying or a friend who went quiet after a plan. The newer 🫥 dotted-line face has picked up that same 'gone invisible' meaning and now shows up in the same texts. This page covers both sides: the literal ghost and its apparition cousins, the Halloween cast 👻 usually appears with, the ghosting-and-disappearing emoji people actually paste to call it out (👋 💨 🚫 📵), playful non-scary ghost moments like costumes and classic arcade ghosts, and spooky kaomoji for plain-text scares.
