Bear Kaomoji

Copy and paste bear kaomoji — cute Japanese bear text faces with round ʕ ʔ ears. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Bear kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built from the round bear brackets ʕ ʔ and a ᴥ snout. A bear kaomoji such as ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ or ʕ ꈍᴥꈍʔ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames. Browse by mood below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "bear kaomoji" or "kaomoji bear," every bear here is free to copy and paste.
