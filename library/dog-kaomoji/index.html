Dog Kaomoji

Copy and paste dog kaomoji — cute Japanese puppy text faces with floppy ears and paws. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Dog kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built around a dog's snout ᴥ or ▼ and floppy ∪ ears. A dog kaomoji such as ໒(＾ᴥ＾)七 or U・ᴥ・U is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames. Browse by mood below and click any face to copy it instantly.
