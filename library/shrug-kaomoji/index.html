The shrug kaomoji ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ — the "smugshrug" — is the classic text face for "I don't know" and "whatever." It is plain Unicode text (the katakana ツ makes the face), so it pastes cleanly into Slack, Discord, chat, comments, and bios with no image.
