Peace Emoji

The peace sign hand for photos, the dove and flag for anti-war and harmony posts, plus meditation and calm-vibes emoji for bios and captions. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The ✌️ victory hand is one of the most-used photo-pose emoji on the internet, thrown up in selfies and group shots as a quick, friendly ‘peace out.’ Alongside it sits a whole family of peace-themed picture emoji with a different register: 🕊️ the dove and 🏳️ the white flag carry anti-war and ceasefire symbolism, while ☯️ yin-yang and 🧘 meditation emoji lean into calm and inner harmony instead. This page collects that full picture-emoji set — the victory-hand gesture in every skin tone, dove and flag symbolism, harmony and meditation emoji, and peace-sign kaomoji — plus ready-made combo sets to copy and paste.
