Angel Emoji

Baby angel, guardian angel, and heaven imagery for tributes, memorials, and blessings — separate from the smiling-face-with-halo meme. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The angel emoji 👼 pictures a baby-faced cherub with wings, and its most literal use is for a baby or young child — including the gentle term 'angel baby' that families use for a child lost during pregnancy or infancy. As a guardian angel, the same emoji signals someone watching over you for protection, luck, or blessing, often paired with a shield 🛡️, cross ✝️, or prayer beads 📿. It's also one of the most common emoji in memorial and 'in loving memory' posts, standing in for a parent, grandparent, pet, or friend who has passed — usually alongside a dove 🕊️, folded hands 🙏, bouquet 💐, candle 🕯️, or headstone 🪦. That's a different job from 😇, the smiling-face-with-halo emoji built around an 'acting innocent' meme — this page stays on 👼 and the wings, heaven, guardian, and tribute imagery around it. Grab a single symbol below or copy a ready-made combo for a memorial caption, a guardian-angel post, or an angel-baby tribute.
