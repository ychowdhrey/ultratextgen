The star emoji ⭐ is one of the internet's most practical picture emoji — a single tap builds a ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ five-star review, rates a product, or crowns a favorite in a caption. This page collects the full picture-emoji star family, from the ⭐ rating star to the 🌟 glowing star, ✨ sparkles, and 🌠 shooting star, plus night-sky companions and star-eyed kaomoji like (☆ω☆). These are full-color emoji that render as little pictures — if you want plain-text Unicode stars like ★ and ☆ that paste anywhere as characters, see the star symbols collection at /library/star-symbols/ (kaomoji and ASCII star art live on their own linked pages too).
