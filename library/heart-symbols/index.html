FAQ

Heart Symbol & Heart Emoji Meanings

What does each heart color mean?

Color is the main thing separating one heart emoji from another, and most colors have settled into a shared meaning: ❤️ red is classic romantic love; 🧡 orange is warmth and care without the romance; 💛 yellow reads as friendship and happiness; 💚 green covers nature, health and — in the older idiom — jealousy; 💙 blue is trust, loyalty and platonic affection; 💜 purple carries compassion and, for millions of K-pop fans, fandom love; 🖤 black is dark humor, grief or a goth aesthetic rather than hate; 🤍 white is purity, peace and minimalist aesthetics; 🤎 brown is earthy and comforting; and the pink family (💗 💓 💕) reads as sweet, growing affection. None of this is defined by Unicode — it is convention, and it shifts by platform, age group and country.

What does the yellow heart 💛 mean?

💛 is the friendship heart. In everyday use it signals platonic affection, warmth and happiness — the heart you send a friend rather than a partner. It also carries a separate, specific meaning on Snapchat, where a yellow heart beside a name means you and that person are currently each other's #1 best friend. That is Snapchat's own system, not the general one — see the Snapchat heart colors breakdown for the full progression.

What does the purple heart 💜 mean?

💜 has three common readings. Generally it stands for compassion, admiration and something a little tender or mystical. In the United States it also refers to the Purple Heart, the military decoration awarded to service members wounded in action, so it appears in veterans' and memorial posts. And in K-pop it is the fandom heart: BTS's V coined "I purple you" in 2016 to mean trusting and loving someone for a long time, and the color has been shorthand for that ever since.

What does the black heart 🖤 mean?

🖤 is far more often dark humor, deadpan sarcasm or a goth/grunge aesthetic than anything hateful. It is also widely used for grief and mourning, and simply as the heart that matches a monochrome or minimalist feed. Read it from context: next to a joke it is usually irony, next to a tribute it is usually loss.

What does the green heart 💚 mean?

💚 covers nature, growth, health and environmental topics — it is the standard heart for anything plant-based, vegan or sustainability-related. It is also the traditional color of jealousy in English (the "green-eyed monster"), though that reading is much rarer online than the nature one. In sports and national contexts it often just matches team or flag colors.

Do heart colors mean the same thing on every app?

No, and this is the most common source of confusion. The general color meanings hold in ordinary chat and captions across most platforms. Snapchat is the big exception: hearts shown next to a friend's name there are generated by Snapchat itself and encode how long you have been each other's #1 best friend, so a 💛 there is a status indicator rather than something the other person sent you. Rendering differs too — the same codepoint is drawn differently by Apple, Google, Samsung and Microsoft, so a heart can look noticeably different on the recipient's screen.

What is the difference between a heart emoji (💕) and a text heart (♥)?

💕 💖 ❤️ are emoji: they render large and in fixed color on every device, so they stand out. ♥ ♡ ❥ ❦ are Unicode text characters — they take on the same color and size as the text around them, which makes them the subtler choice for a bio, a username or a display name. Both are real characters rather than images, so both copy and paste anywhere plain text is accepted.

Why does a heart show up as a box (□) or a question mark?

That is a missing glyph, usually called tofu. The device receiving your text does not have a font covering that character, so it draws a placeholder box instead. Newer or rarer hearts (🩷 🩵 🩶, added in Unicode 15) are the most likely to fail on older phones and desktop apps. If it matters that everyone sees it, use a long-established heart like ♥ ❤️ 💕 instead.

How do I type a heart without copying it?

On Windows, hold Alt and type 3 on the numeric keypad for ♥, or press Windows + . to open the emoji picker. On a Mac, press Control + Command + Space for the emoji and symbol viewer. On iPhone and Android, add the emoji keyboard and search "heart". Copying from this page is usually faster, and it is the only reliable route for the rarer text hearts like ❥ and ❦.

Are these heart symbols free to use?

Yes. These are standard Unicode characters, not images or fonts, so there is nothing to license and no attribution needed — use them in bios, posts, names, documents and commercial work freely. What each platform draws for a given character is that vendor's own artwork, but the character you copy is universal.
