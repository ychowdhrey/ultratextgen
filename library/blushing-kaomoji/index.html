Blushing kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are shy, bashful Japanese-style text faces — rosy cheeks drawn with characters like ⁄ ⁄ and 〃 to show a flustered, embarrassed, or lovestruck moment. A blushing kaomoji such as (⁄ ⁄•⁄ω⁄•⁄ ⁄) or ( 〃▽〃) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and messages.
