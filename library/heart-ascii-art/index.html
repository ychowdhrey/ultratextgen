Heart ASCII Art

Original heart pictures drawn entirely in plain text — one-line hearts, small hearts, and big multi-line pieces. Click Copy on any card.

Heart ASCII art draws a heart shape entirely out of keyboard characters — from the two-keystroke classic <3 to large multi-line hearts built from asterisks, colons, and slashes. One-line hearts paste cleanly anywhere; the bigger multi-line pieces keep their shape only where text renders in a fixed-width (monospace) font, such as Discord code blocks, terminals, and plain-text editors. Every piece below is an original drawing — click Copy to grab it with its line breaks and spacing intact.
