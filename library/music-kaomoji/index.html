Music Kaomoji

Copy and paste music kaomoji — singing and dancing Japanese text faces trailing musical notes. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Music kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces humming, singing, and dancing along — usually trailing a ♪ or ♫. A music kaomoji such as ♪(´▽｀) or ♪┏(・o･)┛♪ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and chats. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "music kaomoji" or "kaomoji music," every face here is free to copy and paste.
