Flower Kaomoji

Copy and paste flower kaomoji — cute Japanese text faces framed with blossoms. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Flower kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces decorated with flower characters like ✿ and ❀ for a soft, springtime look. A flower kaomoji such as (◕‿◕✿) or (◠‿◠✿) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "flower kaomoji" or "kaomoji flower," every face here is free to copy and paste.
