Flower Kaomoji

Copy and paste flower kaomoji — cute Japanese text faces decorated with ❀ and ✿ flowers, flower crowns, and blooms. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Flower kaomoji (also spelled flower kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces decorated with ❀, ✿, and ❁ flowers — soft, cottagecore, and full of cheer. A flower kaomoji like (◠‿◠✿) or (◕‿◕)❀ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames.
