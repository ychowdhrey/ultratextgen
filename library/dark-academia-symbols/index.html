Dark Academia Symbols

Old books, candlelight, quills, and vintage scholarly motifs. Copy and paste dark academia aesthetic symbols for bios, captions, and usernames.

Dark academia is a literary, old-world aesthetic built around leather-bound books, candlelight, ink, and the quiet of an old library — tweed and oil lamps rather than neon. These symbols capture that mood: open books 📖, flickering candles 🕯️, quills 🪶, and vintage ornaments for a bio, caption, or username that reads more Oxford reading room than TikTok trend.
