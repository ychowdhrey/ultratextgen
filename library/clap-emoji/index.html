Clap Emoji

The 👏 clapping hands emoji for praise, applause, and the word-by-word emphasis meme, plus skin tone variants and one-click combo sets. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The clap emoji 👏 is one of the most-used hand gestures on the internet, and it does two very different jobs. Used once at the end of a sentence, it reads as straightforward applause or praise — nice work, well said, congrats. But dropped between every word in a sentence, it becomes the internet's emphasis meme: 'THIS 👏 IS 👏 NOT 👏 OKAY' or 'STOP 👏 TALKING 👏 OVER 👏 ME' turns 👏 into punctuation that mimics someone clapping out each syllable for emphasis. This page covers both uses — the plain applause glyph in every skin tone, ready-made emphasis-format combos you can copy and swap words into, and pairing emoji for captions, comments, and DMs. If you want the full range of hand gestures instead of just clapping, the hand symbols library linked below has the complete set.
