Skull Emoji & Kaomoji

From the plain 💀 skull to the internet's favorite way to say "I'm dead laughing," plus crossbones, danger signs, and skull kaomoji like x_x. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The skull emoji 💀 is one of the most culturally loaded characters online — it stopped meaning literal death years ago and now mostly means "I'm dead," as in something is so funny you have died laughing, quietly replacing 😂 as the go-to reaction. This page collects the picture-emoji skulls (💀 ☠️), the crossbones and danger symbols they pair with, and single-line skull kaomoji like x_x and (☠‿☠). Copy any glyph on its own, or grab a ready-made combo like 💀😂🤣 for a comment that says "that is so funny I'm dead." Looking for the multi-line ASCII art skull drawn from text characters instead?
