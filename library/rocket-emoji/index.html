🚀 started as a plain space-travel emoji, but on stock and crypto Twitter it took on a life of its own: 'to the moon' became shorthand for a price about to explode upward, and the rocket became the visual shorthand for that bet. It's since spread far past finance — startup founders use it for launches, creators use it for 'we're growing fast,' and it shows up anywhere someone wants to signal momentum or ambition without saying a word.
