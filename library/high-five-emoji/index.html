High Five Emoji

The ✋ high-five slap, 🙌 raised-hands cheer, and every clapping, fist-bump, and confetti symbol for celebrating a win. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

There's no single official 'high five' emoji — the moment is built from a small combo of hands: the raised ✋ palm mid-slap, the 🙌 raised hands thrown up in celebration, and the 🤚 back-of-hand wave that reads as an open palm ready to meet another. This page gathers that whole celebratory cluster — the high-five and raised-hands gestures, the clapping 👏 and fist-bump 👊 moves that round out a team win, congratulations faces and confetti, and kaomoji that mimic the gesture in plain text. Grab a single symbol or copy a ready-made combo for a win, a shoutout, or a 'we did it' moment. If you want the broader deal-and-greeting range instead, the handshake emoji page covers agreements and hellos, and the hand symbols hub browses every hand gesture in one grid.
