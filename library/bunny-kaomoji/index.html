Bunny Kaomoji

Copy and paste bunny kaomoji — cute Japanese rabbit text faces with long ears. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Bunny kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built around rabbit ears — rounded ears like ᐢ and ᕱ, or floppy ears drawn with ／ and ＼. A bunny kaomoji such as (•ㅅ•) or ／(^ ᆺ ^)＼ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly.
