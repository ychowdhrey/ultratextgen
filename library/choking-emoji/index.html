Choking Emoji & Kaomoji

The 😵 knocked-out face, dying-laughing 💀 reactions, choking-under-pressure faces, and X_X-style kaomoji for when you truly cannot breathe. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

There is no single official 'choking' emoji, so the internet improvised around 😵, the face with crossed-out eyes that reads as knocked out cold, breathless, or completely overwhelmed. This page builds out that whole range: literal can't-breathe faces like 😮‍💨 and 😧, the 'I'm dying' meme usage of 💀 and 😂 for when something's so funny you're choking on laughter, and the sports-and-gaming slang sense of 'choking' — blowing a lead or missing the clutch moment, paired with 😭, 🤡, and 📉. When an emoji can't carry it, X_X-style kaomoji like (x_x) and (=_=) paste that knocked-out, blanked-out look anywhere plain text works.
