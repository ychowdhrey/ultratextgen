Confused Kaomoji

Question marks, tilted heads, mismatched eyes and baffled shrugs. Tap a puzzled face to copy it into any chat.

Confusion in a kaomoji comes from what sits around the face as much as the face itself. A trailing ? or ？ turns any blank look into a question. Eyes of two sizes, as in o_O or (⊙_☉), show a double take. Spiral or @ eyes mean the head is spinning, and arms raised in ┐ ┌ are a shrug that says no idea. A ゞ after the face is a hand scratching the back of the head, which is the classic anime gesture for being lost. Send one when a message makes no sense, when instructions contradict themselves, or when you simply did not follow. The 😕 🤨 emoji versions are on the confused emoji page linked below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Confused Kaomoji, Answered

What does o_O mean?

It is a double take. One eye is wide and the other is narrow, as if the reader is staring at something odd and not sure they read it right. O.o and (⊙_☉) mean the same thing.

What is the ゞ at the end of (・・;)ゞ?

ゞ is a Japanese kana repetition mark. In kaomoji its shape stands in for a hand raised to the back of the head, which in anime means confusion or mild embarrassment.

Is ┐(￣ヘ￣)┌ the same as the shrug ¯\_(ツ)_/¯?

Both are shrugs with arms raised. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ is relaxed and says it does not matter. ┐(￣ヘ￣)┌ has a frowning mouth, so it reads as being stumped rather than unbothered.
