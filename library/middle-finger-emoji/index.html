Middle Finger Emoji & Kaomoji

The 🖕 middle finger emoji, dismissive eye-rolls, steam-from-the-nose frustration, and defiant kaomoji, gathered in one place. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The middle finger emoji 🖕 (formally 'Reversed Hand With Middle Finger Extended') is a rude hand gesture that renders on most modern platforms, and like other hand emoji it accepts the five Fitzpatrick skin-tone modifiers. This page collects it alongside genuinely related frustration and defiance reactions — the 🙄 eye roll, the 😤 steam-from-the-nose huff, the 💢 anger symbol — plus 🤦 facepalms and a set of annoyed, defiant kaomoji that paste anywhere plain text works.
