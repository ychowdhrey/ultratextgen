Star ASCII Art

Original star pictures drawn entirely in plain text — sparkles, shooting stars, and a giant five-point star. Click Copy on any card.

Star ASCII art builds star shapes out of ordinary keyboard characters — asterisks, slashes, dashes, and underscores. The small one-line pieces paste cleanly into any bio or chat, while the bigger multi-line stars keep their points sharp only where text renders in a fixed-width (monospace) font, such as Discord code blocks, terminals, and plain-text files. Every piece below is an original drawing — click Copy to grab it with its spacing and line breaks intact. For single star characters like ★ and ✦, see the star symbol collection.
