Pride & LGBTQ Symbols

The Rainbow and Transgender Pride flags, core identity signs, and heart-color combos for the flags Unicode hasn't given their own emoji yet. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

Unicode has only ever standardized two dedicated Pride flag emoji: the Rainbow flag and the Transgender flag. Every other flag in the wider LGBTQ community — bisexual, pansexual, lesbian, non-binary, genderfluid, asexual, and more — has no single official character, so people represent them the way text always improvises around a gap: with a run of color-matched heart emoji standing in for each flag's stripes. That workaround is common enough that it helped motivate Unicode 15.0 adding the pink, light blue, and grey hearts in 2023. This page collects the two real flag emoji, the core gender and orientation signs, and a full set of heart-color combos for the flags that still need one. Demand for this page peaks every June for Pride Month, so it is built and live well ahead of that — not scrambled together during it.
