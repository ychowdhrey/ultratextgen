Anime Symbols

Sparkle borders, battle marks, and fandom name frames anime fans copy into bios, usernames, and edits. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

Anime symbols are less about a single alphabet and more about a look — the sparkle accents of a shoujo edit, the crossed blades of a shonen opening, the moon-and-star hush of a magical-girl scene. This library collects those decorative building blocks separately from the site's other Japanese-language pages: for kanji, hiragana, and katakana with real meanings, see Japanese Symbols; for emoticon faces like (＾▽＾), see Text Faces & Kaomoji. Here, the focus is purely aesthetic and fandom-facing — accents to dress up a name, plus ready-made frames you can paste your own name into. New anime seasons premiere on a real, recurring quarterly calendar (winter, spring, summer, fall simulcasts), so this page is built to stay evergreen through every one of them, not just whatever show is trending today.
