The thinking emoji 🤔 is internet shorthand for 'hmm, let me consider that' — hand on the chin, eyebrow cocked, judgment still pending. This page gathers the whole pondering range: the skeptical squint of 🧐, the raised eyebrow of 🤨, the honest 🤷 shrug for when you truly have no idea, and the 💭 thought balloon and 🧠 brain for a big-brain moment. When an emoji won't carry the exact vibe, classic thinking kaomoji like (・_・?) and ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ paste anywhere plain text works.
