Sad Kaomoji

Copy and paste sad kaomoji — Japanese text faces for gloom, disappointment, sulking, and lonely moods. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Sad kaomoji (also spelled sad kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces for low moods — disappointment, gloom, sulking, and loneliness — without the full tears of a crying kaomoji. A sad kaomoji like (´･_･`) or (◞‸◟) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and chats.
