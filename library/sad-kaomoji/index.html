Sad Kaomoji

Copy and paste sad kaomoji — downcast, disappointed, and gloomy Japanese text faces. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Sad kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces for a low mood that stops short of full tears — disappointment, sighing, loneliness, and quiet gloom. A sad kaomoji such as (´･_･`) or (´ω`) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and messages. Browse by feeling below and click any face to copy it instantly. However you search it — sad kaomoji or kaomoji sad — every face here is free to copy and paste. For tears and sobbing, see the crying kaomoji page.
