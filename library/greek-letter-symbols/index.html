This library gathers the full Greek alphabet in uppercase and lowercase, the final-form sigma, and the variant letterforms used as mathematical symbols, so you can copy alpha, beta, delta, sigma, omega, pi, and the rest without hunting through a character map.

Lunate Sigma

Lunate Sigma and Its Editorial Forms

The lunate sigma ϲ is the C-shaped sigma of late Greek and Byzantine writing. The reversed and dotted forms are critical signs from old editions of Greek texts, and ϶ is the reversed lunate epsilon used in math.
