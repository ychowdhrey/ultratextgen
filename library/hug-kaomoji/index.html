Hug Kaomoji

Copy and paste hug kaomoji — warm, open-armed Japanese text faces ready to hug. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Hug kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces with arms thrown open for a hug — often reaching out with a っ or づ. A hug kaomoji such as (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ or ⊂(・ω・*⊂) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and messages. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "hug kaomoji" or "kaomoji hug," every face here is free to copy and paste.
