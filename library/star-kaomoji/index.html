Star Kaomoji

Copy and paste star kaomoji — starry-eyed and dazzled Japanese text faces. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Star kaomoji (also spelled kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built around the star symbol ★ — star-shaped eyes, dazzled wonder, and starstruck excitement. A star kaomoji such as (★ω★) or (☆▽☆) is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames. Browse by style below and click any face to copy it instantly. Whether you search "star kaomoji" or "kaomoji star," every face here is free to copy and paste.
