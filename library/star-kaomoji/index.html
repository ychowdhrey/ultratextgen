Star Kaomoji

Copy and paste star kaomoji — Japanese text faces with starry eyes, sparkles, and star-struck excitement. Click any face to copy it instantly.

Star kaomoji (also spelled star kamoji) are Japanese-style text faces built around ★ and ☆ — for excitement, wonder, and starry-eyed hype. A star kaomoji like (★ω★) or ☆*:.｡.o(≧▽≦)o.｡.:*☆ is plain Unicode text, so it pastes cleanly into bios, captions, comments, and usernames.
