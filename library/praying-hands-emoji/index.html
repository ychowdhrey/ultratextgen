Praying Hands Emoji & Kaomoji

Prayer, gratitude, please, or high-five — the 🙏 emoji somehow means all of them. Grab folded hands, palms up, bowing kaomoji, and ready-made combos. Click any symbol to copy it instantly.

The praying hands emoji 🙏 is one of the most argued-over characters on the internet — some read it as prayer, plenty use it to mean thank-you or 'please,' and a stubborn crowd insists it's really two people mid high-five. Unicode officially names it 'Folded Hands,' which conveniently sidesteps the whole debate and lets the emoji carry every meaning at once. This page collects the praying hands emoji and its palms-up cousin 🤲, the faith and reflection symbols that pair with them, classic bowing and pleading kaomoji like m(_ _)m, and one-click combo sets for gratitude, prayer requests, and good-vibes captions. Whichever side of the high-five argument you land on, every symbol below copies with a single tap.
