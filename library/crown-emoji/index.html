Across Platforms

How the Crown Emoji Looks on Each Device

👑 is one of the more consistent emoji across vendors — a gold crown with jewels on essentially every platform — which is part of why it travels so well in bios and display names. Apple's has a slightly deeper gold and a visible red gem; Google's is flatter and more angular.

The pairing to watch is 👑 with 🤴 or 👸, where the human emoji carry skin-tone variants and the crown does not. A crown on its own reads as status without attaching it to a person, which is usually what a display name wants.

For a crown that is plain text and survives fields that reject emoji — ♛ ♕ ♚ ♔ — use the chess-piece characters on the nickname symbols page. Those are the ones that pass most game name filters, where 👑 is often rejected.

FAQ

Crown Emoji, Answered

Can I put a crown emoji in my username?

In a display name, usually yes. In an @handle, almost never — handles are ASCII-only on most platforms. Games are stricter still and frequently reject emoji outright; the plain-text crowns ♛ and ♔ pass far more often, and are on the nickname symbols page.

What is the difference between 👑 and ♛?

👑 is a full-colour picture emoji. ♛ is a plain text character — the black chess queen — that renders in whatever font surrounds it. The second one pastes into far more places, including most game name fields, which is why decorated nicknames tend to use it.

What does the crown emoji mean?

Status, winning, or being someone's favourite — “queen”, “king”, “GOAT”. It is one of the most common decorations in gaming display names and Instagram bios precisely because it reads the same way in almost every culture.

Does the crown emoji have skin tones?

No. 👑 is an object, so it has no skin-tone variants. The person emoji it is often paired with — 🤴 and 👸 — do, which is why those two can look different from what the sender picked if the recipient's device is old.

Why does my crown emoji show as a box?

The device rendering it has no emoji font covering that character. 👑 is an old, widely-supported emoji so this is rare — if it happens, the device is very old, and a plain-text ♛ will display where the emoji will not.
