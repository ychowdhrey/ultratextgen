Ways to use the dot-to-dot alphabet

  • Count & connect Say each number out loud while connecting the dots in order — number recognition and letter recognition in one activity.
  • Then color Once the letter is revealed, the thin outline leaves room for crayons, markers, and colored pencils.
  • Classroom Print the whole alphabet for a center activity, or one letter for the letter of the week.
