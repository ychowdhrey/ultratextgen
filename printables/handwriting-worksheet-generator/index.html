The seven difficulty levels

One slider raises or lowers three things at once — how thick the guide is, how close the dots sit, and how dark the letters are — so the challenge grows smoothly as a child’s control improves. Start easy, then move up a single notch at a time.

  • 1 · Solid model Full dark letters to trace right on top — the gentlest start.
  • 2 · Bold dotted Thick, closely-spaced dots that are easy to join up.
  • 3 · Fine dotted Thinner dots with a little more space between them.
  • 4 · Dashed Broken dashes — more line for the child to complete.
  • 5 · Faded ghost Light gray letters to write over without a hard outline.
  • 6 · Faint guide A barely-there outline — almost writing solo.
  • 7 · Blank line Just the ruled line — writing from memory.
