How the cursive r works — joins, family, and the usual mistake

R is an undercurve-family letter (i, j, p, r, s, t, u, w), and with s, one of the two trickiest in it. A tall, pointed shoulder turns r into a broken n — keep the jog small and at the midline.

Cursive R — FAQ

Undercurve from the baseline up to the midline. At the top, make a small shoulder: a slight dip and a jog to the right. Slide down to the baseline and exit with an undercurve. r's shoulder is subtle — barely a bump. Overdrawing it is what makes r the letter most adults say they never mastered.
