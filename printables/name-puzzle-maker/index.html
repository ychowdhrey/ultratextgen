Getting the most from a name puzzle

  • Cardstock first Thin paper tears at the cut lines fast — cardstock survives little hands and repeat play.
  • Laminate to reuse Laminate before cutting and the pieces become a durable puzzle you can use for weeks.
  • Start in order Lay the pieces left to right the first few times, then shuffle them once the name is familiar.
  • Keep it stored A small envelope or zip pouch keeps each name's pieces together between sessions.
