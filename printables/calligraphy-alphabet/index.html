Blackletter vs. modern script calligraphy

Blackletter (also called gothic or Old English) is the dense, angular hand of medieval manuscripts — dramatic capitals and heavy verticals. Modern script is lighter and flowing, closer to elegant handwriting. This page gives you both: an ornate blackletter model to trace, plus script variants for every letter.
